What & Why JEE Main & Advanced?

Simplifying the entrance gateway to premier engineering institutes in India.

⚙️ What is JEE Main & Advanced?

JEE Main is the primary national entrance exam conducted by NTA for admission into NITs, IIITs, and CFTIs. It also serves as the eligibility test for JEE Advanced.

JEE Advanced is conducted by one of the 7 zonal IITs exclusively for top JEE Main qualifiers to secure admission into the prestigious Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

🚀 Why 12+ Lakh Students Appear?

JEE is the ultimate benchmark for engineering aspirants in India, granting access to world-class faculty, cutting-edge labs, global research opportunities, and top-tier placements.

It tests deep analytical thinking, problem-solving speed, and fundamental mastery of Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.
