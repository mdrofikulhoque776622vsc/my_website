What & Why NEET UG?

Simplifying the entry gateway to medical education in India.

🩺 What is NEET UG?

NEET UG (National Eligibility cum Entrance Test) is the single national-level entrance exam in India for students who wish to study undergraduate medical courses (MBBS), dental (BDS), AYUSH (BAMS, BHMS), and veterinary sciences.

It is conducted once every year by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

🚀 Why 20+ Lakh Students Appear?

NEET UG is the only single window entry for every government and private medical college across India, including premier institutes like AIIMS and JIPMER.

It guarantees fair merit-based selection, high career security, and the prestigious opportunity to serve society as a doctor.
